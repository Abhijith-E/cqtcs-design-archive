## 🗂️ Directory Overview

### 1. `logos/`
Contains official branding materials and partner logos used across all publications.
- **PascalCase** naming is used here (e.g., `ChristUniversityLogo.svg`, `IitMandiLogo.jpg`).

### 2. `posters/`
High-quality posters designed for various department needs.
- `events/`: Posters for guest lectures, symposiums, and departmental events.
- `publications/`: Visual summaries of research papers and academic publications.
- `courses/` & `recruitment/`: Posters for academic programs and hiring.

### 3. `newsletter/`
A dedicated section for the *Quanicle* newsletter. Contains its own structure for releases, team profiles (Original and Circular cropped), and specific layout assets.

### 4. `design-drafts/` & `design-materials/`
The working directories for the design team.
- `design-drafts/presentations/`: PowerPoint and PDF drafts of department presentations.
- `design-materials/raw-assets/`: A sandbox for raw images, WhatsApp forwards, and AI-generated conceptual art before they are integrated into final designs.

### 5. `templates/` & `exports/`
- `templates/`: Base files to start new designs while maintaining brand consistency.
- `exports/`: The final, ready-to-publish files (PDFs, PNGs, etc.).

Files follow standardized naming to ensure consistency and cross-platform compatibility:

| Convention        | Example                        | Used For                          |
